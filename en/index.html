About us

HUMAN STEM is a secular, non-partisan, non-profit civil association based in Mexico. We incubate social, environmental and cultural projects; today we work with Proyecto Superman and Programa Miltli.

MissionTo put scientific and technological knowledge at the service of the community, addressing environmental, social and cultural challenges.

VisionTo develop sustainable, long-term solutions with a high impact on the country and its communities.

Core lesson

Handout-based aid is born of noble intentions and changes lives, but it does not solve a society’s problems: supplies run out, one-off support is finite, some problems cannot be solved in a day, and it can even harm the community it means to benefit.
